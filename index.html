Privacy Policy

Elder Dragons: Tower Defense, by Bluefox Games · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Elder Dragons: Tower Defense ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. Bluefox Games ("we") is the developer. If you have a question, contact us at anuragkennedy@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-up and no server of our own. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.

Your game progress (levels, stars, heroes, items, settings) is saved on your device. If you choose a player name in the game, it stays on your device and only appears in pictures you decide to share.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example "your dragon egg hatched"). They are created on the phone; nothing is sent to us.

4. Sharing

When you tap "Share" or "Invite", the game opens your phone's share menu with a picture and a short message. You choose where to send it. Invite, gift and challenge codes are created and checked on your device.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

  • Personalized ads: change your consent in Settings → Privacy options (where available) or reset/delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the game to delete its local save. To delete a cloud save, remove the game's data in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings.
  • Questions: email anuragkennedy@gmail.com.

7. Security

Data handled by Google services is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your local save is protected against editing so leaderboards stay fair.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
